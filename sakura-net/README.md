# Sakura Net

A small idle game. Girls wander through a sakura park and you tap them to catch them. Each friend you collect earns hearts per second, and you keep earning while you're away (up to 8 hours).

Open `index.html` in a browser to play. Progress is saved in your browser's localStorage.

- 15 characters in five rarities (N, R, SR, SSR, UR). Rarer girls move faster and glow.
- Catching the same girl again raises her hearts per second.
- Upgrades: Bigger Net, Sakura Mochi Lure, Lucky Omamori, Helper Maid (catches girls for you), Fan Club (multiplies all income).
